About Angavu Metrics

Clarity, brightness, insight — the meaning behind our name and the standard behind our work.

Our Story

Making Evidence Useful

Angavu Metrics is a specialist consultancy dedicated to helping organisations design, implement, and evaluate programmes that deliver measurable, sustainable impact. We work at the intersection of monitoring and evaluation, impact research, policy advisory, and institutional strengthening — serving NGOs, development partners, and government institutions across Africa and globally.

The name Angavu is drawn from Swahili and carries meanings of clarity, brightness, and insight. It reflects our founding conviction: that better evidence, presented with clarity, leads to better decisions — and better outcomes for the communities that development programmes serve.

We exist to close a persistent gap in the development ecosystem: the disconnect between evidence production and evidence use. Too often, evaluations are filed rather than applied. Too often, MEAL systems report upward but fail to feed learning back into programmes. Angavu Metrics is built to change that — delivering evidence that decision-makers can trust, understand, and act on with confidence.

Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, we are registered with the Registrar of Companies and Kenya's NGO Coordination Board. We maintain an academic partnership with the University of Nairobi, connecting rigorous research capacity with practical development expertise.

Vision

Where We're Headed

A world where development decisions are consistently informed by rigorous evidence, institutional systems are resilient and adaptive, and the impact of every programme investment is clearly understood and equitably distributed.

Mission

What We Do Every Day

To provide NGOs, development partners, and government institutions with the high-quality monitoring and evaluation, impact research, and policy advisory services they need to design better programmes, demonstrate results, strengthen systems, and improve outcomes for the communities they serve.
