# STEP 6 — CAPABILITY STATEMENT: Angavu Metrics

*A concise, proposal-ready summary for annexing to bids, expressions of interest, and partner introductions.*

---

## Who We Are

Angavu Metrics is a Nairobi-headquartered consultancy specialising in monitoring and evaluation, impact research, and policy advisory for the development sector. We partner with NGOs, development agencies, and government institutions to design evidence-driven programmes, build robust MEAL systems, conduct rigorous evaluations, and translate findings into actionable recommendations. The name *Angavu* — Swahili for clarity and brightness — captures our core commitment: making evidence accessible, practical, and useful for the decisions that matter.

---

## Our Value to Partners

- **Methodological rigour:** We apply mixed-methods research, experimental and quasi-experimental designs, and theory-based evaluation approaches that meet the highest international standards.
- **Contextual depth:** We understand the institutional, political, and community dynamics that shape programme outcomes across African and international contexts.
- **Decision-ready deliverables:** Our reports, dashboards, and briefs are designed to inform decisions — not to sit on shelves.
- **Partnership orientation:** We work alongside client teams, building capacity and ownership rather than creating dependency.
- **Accountability and transparency:** We are open about our methods, assumptions, and findings — including what the evidence does not show.

---

## Core Services

- **Programme Design & Implementation Support** — Theory of change development, results frameworks, baseline assessments, and adaptive management support.
- **Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL/MEL)** — End-to-end MEAL system design, digital data collection, real-time monitoring, and learning reviews.
- **Impact Evaluations & Surveys** — Baseline, midterm, and endline studies; RCTs; quasi-experimental designs; mixed-methods evaluations aligned with OECD-DAC criteria.
- **Government & Public-Sector Technical Assistance** — M&E frameworks for government, institutional capacity assessments, policy gap analyses, and reform roadmaps.
- **Capacity Building & Training** — MEAL training, research methods workshops, data analysis skills, and embedded coaching for programme and government staff.
- **Policy Analysis, Research & Advisory** — Evidence synthesis, political economy analysis, policy briefs, and knowledge products for decision-makers.

## Selected Technical Capabilities

- Design and operationalisation of MEAL systems for complex, multi-country programmes
- Large-scale household surveys using probability sampling
- Randomised controlled trials (RCTs) and quasi-experimental impact evaluations
- Mixed-methods research (quantitative surveys, KIIs, FGDs, participatory methods)
- Theory of change development and testing
- OECD-DAC criteria application across evaluation types
- Adaptive management and CLA (Collaborating, Learning, Adapting) frameworks
- Systems thinking and complexity-aware monitoring
- Digital data collection (ODK, KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO) and data management
- Statistical analysis and data visualisation (Stata, R, SPSS, Power BI, Tableau)
- Political economy analysis and institutional assessments
- Knowledge management and learning product development

## Geographical Focus

Angavu Metrics is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, with deep expertise across East Africa (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ethiopia, and Somalia). We work across the African continent and are positioned to support assignments globally. Our local presence provides the contextual understanding, institutional relationships, and logistical capacity needed for effective field-based research and evaluation, while our methodological approach and quality standards meet the requirements of international donors and multilateral partners worldwide.
