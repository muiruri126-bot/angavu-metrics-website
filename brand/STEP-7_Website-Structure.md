## 2. Homepage Sections

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline:** "Clarity. Evidence. Impact."
- **Subheading:** "We help NGOs, development partners, and governments design better programmes, measure what matters, and turn evidence into action."
- **Primary CTA:** "Explore Our Services" (links to Services page)
- **Secondary CTA:** "Share Your TOR" (links to Contact/RFP page)
- **Visual:** Abstract data visualisation pattern or a clean SVG animation of the Angavu logo icon elements (rising bars, light beam). No stock photography in the hero.

### Section 2: Service Snapshot
- **Heading:** "What We Do"
- Six service cards in a 3×2 or 2×3 grid, each with:
  - Icon (from a consistent icon set — outlined, minimal)
  - Service name
  - One-sentence description
  - "Learn more →" link to the relevant service page
- Keep descriptions tight — no more than 15 words each.

### Section 3: Our Approach
- **Heading:** "How We Work"
- Three horizontal pillars with icons:
  - **Evidence-Driven** — "We ground every engagement in rigorous, context-sensitive data."
  - **Participatory** — "We engage stakeholders from question design through to evidence use."
  - **Adaptive** — "We build learning into every process, so programmes evolve with the evidence."
- Optional light background colour (warm white or very pale teal) to differentiate from adjacent sections.

### Section 4: Featured Projects
- **Heading:** "Selected Work"
- 2–3 project cards showing:
  - Project title
  - Client type (e.g., "INGO – East Africa")
  - Sector tags
  - Brief summary (2 sentences)
  - "Read case study →" link
- Initially these can be placeholders until real projects are populated.

### Section 5: Clients & Partners Strip
- **Heading:** "Trusted By" or "Our Partners"
- Horizontal scroll or static logo bar showing client/partner logos.
- Include University of Nairobi logo. Others to be added as partnerships develop.

### Section 6: Testimonials
- **Heading:** "What Our Partners Say"
- 1–3 testimonial cards (initially with placeholders).
- Format: quote text, attribution (name, title, organisation).

### Section 7: Call to Action
- **Heading:** "Ready to Work Together?"
- **Subheading:** "Share your Terms of Reference or get in touch to discuss how Angavu Metrics can support your programme."
- **CTA Button:** "Submit Your TOR / RFP"
- Clean, minimal section with primary brand colour background and white text.

## 3. Content Tone Guidance for Website

### General Principles

The website should be **slightly more accessible** than formal proposals and donor reports, while maintaining professional credibility. The goal is to make Angavu Metrics feel approachable and human without being casual or informal.

**Specific guidance:**

- **Shorter sentences.** Aim for 15–20 words per sentence on the website (vs. 25–30 in formal documents).
- **Active voice, always.** "We design MEAL systems" not "MEAL systems are designed by our team."
- **Second person for CTAs.** "Share your TOR with us" — speak directly to the visitor.
- **First person plural for descriptions.** "We help organisations…" — reinforces the partnership positioning.
- **Avoid acronym soup on landing pages.** Spell out MEAL, MEL, ToC, OECD-DAC on first use. On service detail pages, acronyms can be used more freely after definition.
- **Lead with client benefit, not technical process.** Instead of "We apply mixed-methods research designs combining quantitative and qualitative approaches," try "We combine surveys, interviews, and participatory methods to give you the full picture."
- **Use questions as section headings where appropriate.** E.g., "Need a MEAL system that actually gets used?" — this makes the content feel conversational and client-centred.

### Page-Specific Tone

| Page | Tone | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| **Home** | Confident, inviting, clear | Lead with value proposition. Minimal jargon. |
| **About** | Warm, grounded, credible | Tell the story. Mention Angavu's meaning. Show the human side. |
| **Services** | Precise, solution-oriented | Describe what we do AND what the client gets. |
| **Approach** | Thoughtful, methodical | Slightly more technical. Okay to use frameworks by name. |
| **Case Studies** | Narrative, evidence-led | Tell the story of the engagement: challenge → approach → result. |
| **Resources** | Accessible, insight-led | Briefs and publications should have clear, non-academic titles. |
| **Team** | Professional, personal | Brief bios. Highlight expertise AND human interests. |
| **Contact** | Direct, welcoming | Make it easy. Reduce friction. |
