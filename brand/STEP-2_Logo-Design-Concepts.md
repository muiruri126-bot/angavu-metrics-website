## CONCEPT 1: "Clarity Prism"

### Concept Name & Rationale

**Clarity Prism** draws on the idea of light passing through a prism — transforming raw data into clear, actionable insight. The abstract "A" is formed by converging light beams that rise upward, suggesting both illumination and upward progress. At the base of the "A," a series of ascending data bars ground the mark in measurement and evidence.

This concept directly reflects Angavu's Swahili roots — clarity and brightness — by making light the central visual metaphor. The prism shape also suggests that Angavu Metrics takes complex programme data and refracts it into distinct, understandable elements: findings, recommendations, and evidence for decision-making.

The design communicates partnership by using open, converging lines rather than closed shapes — suggesting collaboration between NGOs, donors, and governments coming together toward shared insight. The upward trajectory of the bars signals progress, growth, and measurable impact.

### Symbolism & Icon Ideas

- **Abstract "A" as a prism/light beam:** Two angled lines converge upward to form the letter "A," with a subtle opening at the apex suggesting a lens or beam of light emanating outward. This represents clarity and illumination.
- **Rising data bars at the base:** Three to four ascending bars of varying heights sit within or beneath the "A" form, representing metrics, measurement, and evidence. The bars increase in height left to right, suggesting growth and progress.
- **Circular dot or light point at the apex:** A small circle or dot at the top of the "A" represents insight — the moment data becomes understanding.
- **Negative space:** The interior of the "A" creates an upward-pointing triangle of white space — a subtle arrow conveying forward movement.

### Layout & Style

- **Primary layout:** Icon sits to the left of the wordmark (horizontal lockup). The icon is compact and works at small sizes.
- **Stacked variant:** Icon above the wordmark for square formats (social media profiles, favicons).
- **Wordmark treatment:**
  - **ANGAVU** in bold, uppercase, tracked slightly wide — the dominant visual element.
  - **Metrics** in a lighter weight, smaller size, lowercase or small caps, positioned below or beside "ANGAVU."
- **Style:** Modern, geometric, minimal. Clean lines, no gradients or shadows. Suitable for print on white paper, embossed on reports, and rendered at small sizes on dashboards.

- **Rationale:** Both fonts are free, web-optimised, and project the right balance of technical credibility and approachability. They avoid the coldness of purely geometric fonts while maintaining the seriousness expected in policy briefs, donor reports, and evaluation deliverables.

## CONCEPT 2: "Impact Horizon"

### Concept Name & Rationale

**Impact Horizon** uses the metaphor of a horizon line — the point where evidence meets action, where data transforms into visible, measurable change. The abstract "A" is constructed from a rising curve or arc (the horizon) with vertical data bars ascending through it, suggesting breakthrough and forward progress.

This concept positions Angavu Metrics as the firm that helps clients see beyond the immediate — to the horizon of long-term, sustainable impact. The horizon line is also a visual metaphor for systems thinking: it suggests the broader landscape within which individual programmes operate.

The open, upward arc communicates optimism and forward movement without being naive — it is grounded by the precision of the data bars beneath it. This duality speaks to Angavu Metrics' promise: rigorous evidence that drives real-world change.

### Symbolism & Icon Ideas

- **Horizon arc forming the crossbar of the "A":** Instead of a straight crossbar, the "A" features a gentle upward arc — a stylised horizon line that suggests looking forward.
- **Ascending data bars:** Three bars of increasing height rise from within the "A" shape, passing through or touching the horizon arc, symbolising evidence-driven progress.
- **Partial lens/eye shape:** The arc can subtly evoke the upper lid of an eye, reinforcing the themes of insight and oversight (monitoring and evaluation).
- **Circular motion implied:** The arc suggests the cyclical nature of MEAL — plan, monitor, evaluate, learn, adapt.

### Layout & Style

- **Primary layout:** Horizontal lockup with icon to the left.
- **Stacked variant:** Icon above wordmark.
- **Wordmark treatment:**
  - **ANGAVU** in bold, with the "A" optionally replaced or visually echoed by the icon mark.
  - **Metrics** in a lighter weight, positioned to the right or below.
- **Style:** Modern and fluid. The arc introduces a single organic curve against otherwise geometric forms, creating visual interest while maintaining professionalism.

### Typography Suggestions

- **Body Font:** **DM Sans** (Google Fonts) — clean, geometric, with a friendly but professional tone. Pairs well with Inter for a cohesive, modern system.
- **Rationale:** Inter is rapidly becoming a standard in professional digital environments, lending instant credibility. DM Sans adds warmth without sacrificing seriousness — ideal for a consultancy that bridges technical rigour and human-centred approaches.

## CONCEPT 3: "Connected Insight"

### Concept Name & Rationale

**Connected Insight** emphasises the interconnected nature of development systems — the linkages between data, people, institutions, and outcomes. The abstract "A" is formed by a network of connected nodes and lines, with the overall shape rising upward. A subtle lens or eye shape is embedded at the intersection point, representing the moment of insight.

This concept reflects Angavu Metrics' systems-thinking approach and its role as a connector — bridging NGOs, government institutions, and development partners. The network visual suggests that impact is not linear but systemic: it emerges from the quality of connections between actors and evidence.

The node-and-line aesthetic also directly references data and analytics — scatter plots, network diagrams, and causal pathways — grounding the brand in the language of evidence and evaluation without being overly technical or cold.

### Symbolism & Icon Ideas

- **Abstract "A" as a node network:** The letter "A" is formed by connected dots (nodes) joined by clean lines. Three to five nodes of varying sizes create the triangular "A" form.
- **Central node as "lens" or "insight point":** The largest node sits at the intersection of the "A" crossbar and the ascending lines — representing the focal point of insight and clarity.
- **Ascending trajectory:** The overall composition tilts or builds upward, with the apex node being the highest point, suggesting progress and aspiration.
- **Data dots:** The smaller nodes evoke data points on a chart, reinforcing measurement and metrics.

### Layout & Style

- **Primary layout:** Icon to the left of wordmark (horizontal). The node-network icon is distinctive and memorable even at small sizes.
- **Stacked variant:** Icon centered above wordmark.
- **Wordmark treatment:**
  - **ANGAVU** in bold, tracked wide, with the first "A" optionally incorporating a subtle node or dot detail.
  - **Metrics** in light weight, spaced beneath or beside.
- **Style:** Minimal, modern, slightly technical. The node aesthetic communicates analytical capability without appearing cold — the varied node sizes add visual rhythm and warmth.

### Typography Suggestions

- **Heading Font:** **Montserrat** (Google Fonts) — geometric, bold, and authoritative. Its wide, stable letterforms project confidence and institutional credibility. Widely used in professional and government-facing communications.
- **Body Font:** **Source Sans 3** (Google Fonts) — as in Concept 1, providing excellent readability for dense analytical content. Pairs well with Montserrat's geometry.
- **Rationale:** Montserrat for headings adds gravitas and brand recognition, while Source Sans 3 for body text ensures long-form reports and briefs remain comfortable to read. Both are free and optimised for web and print.

## LOGO IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

- All three concepts should be developed in **SVG format** for scalability.
- Each concept needs: full-colour, single-colour (dark), single-colour (light/reversed), and monochrome versions.
- Minimum clear space around the logo should equal the height of the "A" icon.
- Minimum size: icon should not be rendered smaller than 24px in digital or 10mm in print.
- The icon should function as a standalone **app icon / favicon** without the wordmark.
