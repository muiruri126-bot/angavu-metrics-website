# STEP 5 — COMPANY PROFILE: Angavu Metrics

## About Us

Angavu Metrics is a specialist consultancy dedicated to helping organisations design, implement, and evaluate programmes that deliver measurable, sustainable impact. We work at the intersection of monitoring and evaluation, impact research, policy advisory, and institutional strengthening — serving NGOs, development partners, and government institutions across Africa and globally.

The name *Angavu* is drawn from Swahili and carries meanings of clarity, brightness, and insight. It reflects our founding conviction: that better evidence, presented with clarity, leads to better decisions — and better outcomes for the communities that development programmes serve.

Angavu Metrics was established to address a persistent gap in the development ecosystem: the need for evaluation and advisory services that are simultaneously rigorous and practical, technically sound and contextually grounded, data-rich and decision-ready. Too often, evidence is produced but not used; reports are delivered but not acted upon; MEAL systems are designed but not sustained. We exist to close that gap — delivering evidence that decision-makers can trust, understand, and act on with confidence.

Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, and registered with the Registrar of Companies and Kenya's NGO Coordination Board, Angavu Metrics operates with the credibility and governance standards expected by international donors and government partners. We maintain an academic partnership with the University of Nairobi, enabling access to cutting-edge research capacity and a pipeline of emerging evaluation professionals.

## Core Service Areas

### Programme Design and Implementation Support

Angavu Metrics supports the full programme cycle — from initial design through implementation to adaptive management. We help clients develop theories of change, define results frameworks, establish baselines, and build implementation plans grounded in evidence and context.

Our programme design work ensures that interventions are:
- Anchored in a robust theory of change that articulates causal pathways and assumptions.
- Informed by baseline evidence and contextual analysis.
- Designed with clear, measurable indicators aligned to donor and government reporting requirements.
- Structured for adaptive management, with built-in feedback loops and learning milestones.

We provide ongoing implementation support to programme teams, helping them navigate the gap between design and delivery — adapting strategies based on emerging evidence, troubleshooting implementation bottlenecks, and ensuring programme fidelity without sacrificing flexibility.

### Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL/MEL)

MEAL system design and management is at the heart of Angavu Metrics' work. We design and deploy MEAL systems that are proportionate, practical, and built to generate evidence that decision-makers actually use.

Our MEAL services include:
- Design and operationalisation of MEAL frameworks, indicator matrices, and data collection tools.
- Development of data management systems, including digital data collection (ODK, KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO) and dashboard integration.
- Real-time monitoring and data quality assurance.
- Accountability mechanisms, including community feedback systems and beneficiary satisfaction tracking.
- Quarterly and annual learning reviews that translate monitoring data into strategic recommendations.
- MEAL capacity assessments and improvement plans for implementing partners.

We apply adaptive management principles, ensuring MEAL systems evolve as programmes mature and as the evidence base grows. Our systems are designed not just to report upward to donors, but to feed learning back into programme teams at every level.

### Impact Evaluations and Baseline/Endline Surveys

Angavu Metrics designs and conducts rigorous impact evaluations, baseline studies, midterm reviews, and endline surveys. We apply the full spectrum of evaluation approaches — from experimental and quasi-experimental designs to theory-based and participatory evaluations — selecting the method best suited to each context and evaluation question.

Our evaluation work includes:
- Randomised controlled trials (RCTs) and quasi-experimental designs (difference-in-differences, propensity score matching, regression discontinuity).
- Theory-based evaluations using contribution analysis, process tracing, and realist evaluation.
- Mixed-methods designs combining quantitative surveys with qualitative data (key informant interviews, focus group discussions, most significant change stories).
- Baseline, midline, and endline surveys with nationally representative or programme-specific sampling.
- Utilisation-focused evaluation approaches that prioritise the information needs of primary intended users.

All evaluations are guided by the OECD-DAC evaluation criteria (relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, and sustainability) and adhere to international evaluation standards and ethics.

Our government advisory work includes:

- Institutional capacity assessments and organisational development plans.
- Policy gap analyses and reform roadmaps.
- Support to county and national government planning and budgeting processes.
- Facilitation of multi-stakeholder platforms for policy dialogue and evidence-informed decision-making.

We bring a collaborative, systems-aware approach to government engagement — working within institutional realities while advocating for evidence-informed practice.

### Capacity Building and Training

Angavu Metrics designs and delivers tailored capacity-building programmes in MEAL, research methods, data analysis, and evidence-informed decision-making. We believe that sustainable impact requires not just good external evidence, but strong internal capacity to generate, interpret, and use data.

Our capacity-building services include:
- Customised MEAL training programmes for programme staff, M&E officers, and senior management.
- Training on data collection tools (ODK, KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO) and data analysis software (Stata, R, SPSS, Excel).
- Research methods workshops covering survey design, sampling, qualitative methods, and mixed-methods approaches.
- Coaching and mentoring for M&E professionals and government statisticians.

Our training is practical, participatory, and grounded in real-world examples from the development sector. We do not deliver generic workshops — every training programme is designed around the client's specific context, capacity gaps, and learning objectives.

### Policy Analysis, Research, and Advisory

Angavu Metrics conducts policy research and analysis to inform evidence-based policy development, reform design, and strategic planning. Our policy advisory services include:

## Approach / Methodology

### Evidence-Driven and Context-Sensitive

Angavu Metrics is built on the conviction that good evidence is necessary but not sufficient — it must be relevant, timely, and presented in ways that decision-makers can use. We begin every engagement by understanding the client's decision-making context: who needs the evidence, what decisions it will inform, and what constraints shape how it can be used. This utilisation-focused orientation ensures that our work generates actionable insight, not shelf reports.

### Participatory and Inclusive

We believe that the best evidence incorporates the perspectives of those closest to the issues being studied — communities, frontline workers, local government officials, and programme participants. Our research and evaluation designs routinely include participatory methods alongside quantitative approaches, ensuring that findings are enriched by the lived experience of those most affected.

### Systems-Thinking and Adaptive Management

Development challenges are complex and interconnected. Angavu Metrics applies systems thinking to understand how programmes interact with broader institutional, social, and political dynamics. We design MEAL systems and evaluations that account for complexity — using adaptive management frameworks, feedback loops, and iterative learning processes to help organisations navigate uncertainty and adjust strategies based on emerging evidence.

**Pillars of Our Approach:**
- **Rigour:** We apply the highest methodological standards appropriate to each context — never over-engineering, never cutting corners.
- **Relevance:** Every deliverable is designed to answer a specific question for a specific audience.
- **Participation:** We engage stakeholders throughout the evidence cycle — from question design through data collection to interpretation and use.
- **Adaptation:** We build learning into every process, enabling organisations to adjust strategies as evidence emerges.
- **Transparency:** We are open about our methods, limitations, and the assumptions underlying our conclusions.
