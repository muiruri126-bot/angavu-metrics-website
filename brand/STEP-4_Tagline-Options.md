# STEP 4 — TAGLINE OPTIONS: Angavu Metrics

---

## Current Tagline Assessment

**"Clarity. Evidence. Impact."**

### Strengths
- Concise and memorable — three words, three pillars.
- Each word maps directly to Angavu's value chain: we bring clarity to complex issues, we ground our work in evidence, and we focus on impact.
- The tricolon structure (three parallel words) is a proven rhetorical device — it creates rhythm and authority.
- Donor-friendly: these three words are immediately understood in the development and MEAL sector.
- Works equally well on a business card, a proposal cover, and a website hero banner.

### Possible Refinements
- The tagline is strong as-is. It could be made slightly more dynamic by introducing a verb or connective, but this risks diluting its elegance.
- The main consideration is whether it feels sufficiently distinctive — many evidence-focused firms use similar language. However, paired with the Angavu name (which is unique and carries its own meaning), it works well as a complementary tagline rather than a standalone brand differentiator.

**Recommendation:** Retain "Clarity. Evidence. Impact." as the primary tagline. It is strong, clear, and donor-ready.

---

## Alternative Tagline Options

The following options are provided in case you wish to explore variations or need alternatives for specific contexts (e.g., a secondary tagline for the website, a strapline for specific service areas, or a longer descriptor).

### 1. "Where Evidence Meets Impact."
Positions Angavu as the bridge between raw data and real-world change. Slightly more narrative than the current tagline — useful for website hero sections or presentation title slides.

### 2. "Data-Driven. Context-Grounded. Impact-Focused."
Emphasises the three-part methodology: rigorous data, deep contextual understanding, and a relentless focus on outcomes. Slightly more specific than the current tagline and highlights the "context" dimension that sets Angavu apart.

### 3. "Evidence for Decisions That Matter."
A client-centred tagline — it speaks to the end use of Angavu's work: informing decisions. "That matter" adds weight without being vague. Works well in government and donor contexts.

### 4. "Measuring What Matters. Illuminating What's Next."
A two-part tagline that connects measurement (MEAL) with forward-looking insight (adaptive management, learning). "Illuminating" ties back to the Angavu meaning of brightness and clarity.

### 5. "Rigour. Relevance. Results."
A tighter tricolon alternative that shifts emphasis toward rigour and practical relevance. Feels slightly more technical and institutional — may resonate well in government and multilateral contexts.

### 6. "Insight That Drives Change."
Short, active, and confident. Positions Angavu's core output (insight) as a catalyst for change. Less descriptive of services but strong as an aspirational brand statement.

### 7. "From Evidence to Action."
Simple, direct, and widely understood in the development sector. Captures the full arc of Angavu's work — from data collection through to actionable recommendations and programme improvement.

### 8. "See Clearly. Act Confidently."
A two-part tagline that speaks to the client's experience: Angavu brings the clarity ("see clearly") that enables confident decision-making. More client-facing and less technical.

### 9. "Turning Data into Direction."
Concise and active. "Direction" implies both strategic guidance and forward movement. Useful as a secondary descriptor or website subheading.

### 10. "Evidence That Builds. Systems That Last."
A more ambitious tagline that speaks to Angavu's dual focus: producing high-quality evidence AND strengthening the systems and institutions that use it. Best suited for contexts where the capacity-building and systems-strengthening work is front and centre.

| Context | Recommended Tagline |
