Privacy Policy

How Angavu Metrics collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, submit a Terms of Reference, or engage our services, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Organisation name and role
  • Information you provide in enquiry forms or emails
  • Terms of Reference or project documents you share with us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and service requests
  • Prepare proposals and project plans
  • Deliver contracted services
  • Improve our website and services

3. Data Protection

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to deliver our services or as required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Project-related data is retained in accordance with our contractual obligations and professional standards.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@angavumetrics.com.

6. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies to ensure proper functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics without your consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@angavumetrics.com.
