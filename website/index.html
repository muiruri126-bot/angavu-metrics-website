How We Work

Our Approach

Every engagement is grounded in three commitments that shape how we design, deliver, and learn.

Evidence-Driven

We ground every engagement in rigorous, context-sensitive data — matching the method to the question, never the other way around.

Participatory

We engage stakeholders from question design through to evidence use — ensuring findings are enriched by the perspectives of those closest to the issues.

Adaptive

We build learning into every process, enabling organisations to adjust strategies as evidence emerges — turning MEAL into a management tool, not just a reporting requirement.
