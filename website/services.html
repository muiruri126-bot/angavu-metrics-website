Our Services

From programme design to impact evaluation, we deliver the rigour and clarity needed to demonstrate results and adapt with confidence.

Service Area

Programme Design & Implementation Support

We support the full programme cycle — from initial design through implementation to adaptive management. We help clients develop theories of change, define results frameworks, establish baselines, and build implementation plans grounded in evidence and context.

Our programme design work ensures that interventions are anchored in evidence, aligned to donor requirements, and structured for adaptive management with built-in feedback loops.

Typical Engagements

  • Theory of change development and refinement workshops
  • Results framework and logframe design
  • Baseline assessments and context analyses
  • Implementation planning and milestone design
  • Adaptive management support and mid-course corrections

Service Area

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL/MEL)

MEAL system design and management is at the heart of our work. We design and deploy MEAL systems that are proportionate, practical, and built to generate evidence that decision-makers actually use — not just filing for compliance.

We apply adaptive management principles, ensuring systems evolve as programmes mature. Our MEAL frameworks feed learning back into programme teams at every level.

What We Deliver

  • MEAL framework and indicator matrix design
  • Digital data collection systems (ODK, KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO)
  • Real-time monitoring and data quality assurance
  • Community feedback and accountability mechanisms
  • Quarterly and annual learning reviews
  • MEAL capacity assessments and improvement plans
  • Dashboard design and data visualisation

Service Area

Impact Evaluations & Surveys

We design and conduct rigorous impact evaluations, baseline studies, midterm reviews, and endline surveys. We apply the full spectrum of evaluation approaches — from experimental and quasi-experimental designs to theory-based and participatory evaluations.

All evaluations are guided by the OECD-DAC evaluation criteria and adhere to international evaluation standards and ethics.

Evaluation Approaches

  • Randomised controlled trials (RCTs) and quasi-experimental designs
  • Theory-based evaluations (contribution analysis, process tracing, realist evaluation)
  • Mixed-methods designs combining surveys with qualitative research
  • Baseline, midline, and endline surveys
  • Utilisation-focused evaluation approaches
  • Most significant change and outcome harvesting

Service Area

Government & Public-Sector Technical Assistance

We provide technical assistance to government ministries, departments, and agencies undertaking institutional reforms, policy development, and performance management improvements. We understand the complexities of public-sector change — the bureaucratic incentive structures, political economy dynamics, and capacity constraints that shape reform outcomes.

What We Deliver

  • Government M&E frameworks and performance management systems
  • Institutional capacity assessments and organisational development plans
  • Policy gap analyses and reform roadmaps
  • Support to county and national government planning and budgeting
  • Multi-stakeholder platforms for policy dialogue and evidence use

Service Area

Capacity Building & Training

We design and deliver tailored capacity-building programmes in MEAL, research methods, data analysis, and evidence-informed decision-making. Sustainable impact requires not just good external evidence, but strong internal capacity to generate, interpret, and use data.

Our training is practical, participatory, and grounded in real-world examples. Every programme is designed around the client's specific context and learning objectives.

Training Areas

  • Customised MEAL training for programme staff and senior management
  • Data collection tools (ODK, KoBoToolbox, SurveyCTO)
  • Data analysis software (Stata, R, SPSS, Excel)
  • Research methods: survey design, sampling, qualitative methods
  • Coaching and mentoring for M&E professionals
  • Learning-by-doing embedded within active evaluations

Service Area

Policy Analysis, Research & Advisory

We conduct policy research and analysis to inform evidence-based policy development, reform design, and strategic planning. We work at the intersection of research and policy, translating complex evidence into practical recommendations for decision-makers.

We apply systems thinking and political economy perspectives to ensure our recommendations are technically sound, politically feasible, and institutionally sustainable.

What We Deliver

  • Sector-specific policy analyses across governance, health, education, livelihoods, climate, and social protection
  • Political economy analyses and stakeholder mapping
  • Evidence synthesis and rapid evidence reviews
  • Policy briefs and knowledge products for decision-makers
  • Support to government policy and strategy development
